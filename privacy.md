# Privacy Policy

This is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

- It accesses the owner's own Google account (Calendar and Sheets) solely to manage the owner's own schedule and expense records.
- It does not collect, store or share data about any other person.
- Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.
